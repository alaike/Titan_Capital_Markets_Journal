Titan Capital Markets · Trading journal blueprint

A professional trading-performance system

Daily entry is a single ticket. Analytics are derived, never typed twice. This page is the specification the workbook and this desk both implement. Set your trader name and Trader ID in Settings so every export and cover sheet is attributed to you.

1. Workbook / database structure

Two stores, one model:

  • Settings — trader name, Trader ID, initial capital, currency, breakeven threshold, default risk %, account name.
  • Trades — one row per execution (partials should be separate rows so nothing is double-counted).
  • Lists — pairs, sessions, setups, positions (dropdown sources).
  • Computed columns are never stored as the source of truth; they are calculated from prices, lots, and timestamps.

Excel companion sheets: 00_ReadMe, 01_Settings, 02_Trade_Log, 03_Dashboard, 04_Breakdowns, 99_Lists.

2. Trade-entry columns and types

Basic — S/N (auto integer), Day of week (auto text), Entry date (date), Entry time (time), Currency pair (list), Position (Buy/Sell), Session (list), Lot size (number > 0), Risk amount (number), Risk % (number).

Management — Entry, Stop loss, Take profit (decimals by pair).

Exit — Exit price, Exit date, Exit time, Duration (auto), Planned RR (auto), Realized RR (auto), Pips (auto).

Result — Gross PnL (auto or broker override), Commission/swap, Net PnL, Result (Win/Loss/BE/Open), Balance after trade (running).

Process (recommended extras) — Exit reason, Followed plan, Market condition, Emotion, Timeframe, Ticket, Chart URL, MAE/MFE, Notes.

3. Formulas and calculation logic

Pip size

JPY pairs → 0.01  XAUUSD → 0.10  all other FX → 0.0001

Direction

Buy / Long = +1  Sell / Short = −1

Pips

result_pips = (exit − entry) × direction ÷ pip_size SL_pips = |entry − SL| ÷ pip_size TP_pips = |TP − entry| ÷ pip_size

Monetary PnL (account currency, USD-style)

raw = (exit − entry) × direction × contract_size × lots XXXUSD & XAUUSD: PnL = raw USDXXX: PnL = raw ÷ exit crosses: estimated as pips × $10 × lots (override with broker PnL) Net PnL = (override OR estimate) − commission contract_size = 100 oz (XAU) or 100,000 (FX)

Risk-to-reward

Planned RR = TP_pips ÷ SL_pips Realized RR = result_pips ÷ SL_pips (negative when the trade loses) If SL_pips = 0 → RR is blank (invalid stop)

Duration

duration = (exit_date + exit_time) − (entry_date + entry_time) Displayed as days, hours, minutes. Negative durations are rejected.

Day of week

WEEKDAY(entry_date) → Monday … Sunday (from the entry calendar date)

Win / Loss / Breakeven

Open = missing exit price or missing exit date BE = |Net PnL| ≤ breakeven_threshold Win = Net PnL > threshold Loss = Net PnL < −threshold

Running balance

Sort closed trades by exit datetime, then S/N balance_0 = initial_capital balance_i = balance_{i−1} + net_pnl_i Open trades do not move the balance.

Dashboard metrics (closed trades only, after filters)

Win rate = wins ÷ closed × 100 Win rate excl. BE = wins ÷ (wins + losses) × 100 Gross profit = Σ positive net PnL Gross loss = |Σ negative net PnL| Profit factor = gross profit ÷ gross loss if gross loss = 0 and gross profit > 0 → ∞ if both = 0 → — Average win = mean of winning PnL Average loss = mean of losing PnL Largest win/loss = max / min net PnL Expectancy = total net PnL ÷ closed count Max drawdown = max peak-to-trough decline on the equity curve

Filters never double-count: each trade has one id; aggregations are simple sums over the filtered set.

4. Dashboard layout and KPI structure

Row 1 — Trades, Win rate, Total PnL, Profit factor, Equity.

Row 2 — Wins, Losses, Breakevens, Largest win, Largest loss, Average win, Average loss, Expectancy.

Insight strip — best/worst pair, day, session, setup from the filtered set.

Charts — equity, outcomes, cumulative PnL, sessions, pairs, weekdays, setups.

Tape — last ten closed trades.

5. Charts and what they communicate

  • Equity curve — path of capital; slope and depth of drawdowns.
  • Cumulative PnL — same path without the starting capital (pure performance).
  • Outcome mix — share of wins, losses, scratches.
  • PnL by pair / day / setup / session — where expectancy actually lives.
  • Win rate by pair / day / setup — hit rate independent of size.
  • Monthly bars — calendar edge, not trade-count edge.
  • RR histogram — whether realized RR matches the planned 1:1 / 1:2 process.

6. Interactive filters

Pair, weekday, session, setup, date range (from entry date). All KPIs, charts, and breakdown tables read the same filtered closed-trade set. Open trades are excluded from performance math and listed separately. Choosing one pair (or one setup, or Monday) recomputes trades, win rate, PnL, profit factor, average win/loss, largest win/loss for that slice only.

7. Conditional formatting

  • Positive PnL / pips / RR → green. Negative → red. Zero/BE → muted.
  • Buy badge green, Sell badge red, Open badge brass.
  • KPI cards take the same colour language.
  • Quality-control flags on the ticket: stop on the wrong side of price is an error; TP on the wrong side is a warning; risk-from-stop vs typed risk that diverges > 15% is a warning.

8. Data validation / dropdowns

Pairs: AUDCHF, AUDJPY, AUDCAD, AUDUSD, AUDNZD, EURGBP, EURCHF, EURJPY, EURCAD, EURUSD, EURNZD, EURAUD, GBPCHF, GBPJPY, GBPCAD, GBPUSD, GBPNZD, GBPAUD, NZDCHF, NZDJPY, NZDCAD, NZDUSD, CADJPY, CADCHF, USDCHF, USDJPY, USDCAD, XAUUSD, CHFJPY, plus Other.

Setups: Safety trade, H1 5050 Bounce, H4 50505 Bounce, Other.

Position: Buy/Long, Sell/Short. Session: Asian, Tokyo, London, New York.

Lots > 0. Prices > 0. Exit datetime ≥ entry datetime. Stop must sit on the protective side of entry.

9. Visual design

Night-ink field, brass rules, serif wordmark, mono figures. KPI cards use registration-mark corners. The order ticket on the right is the only place numbers are “performed” while you type — the log stays quiet. Density is terminal-like; colour is reserved for P&L and risk.

10. Quality-control checks

  • Required: pair, position, setup, session, entry date/time/price, stop, lots.
  • Stop side must match direction (Buy: SL < entry; Sell: SL > entry).
  • Take-profit side warned, not blocked (you may trail or scale).
  • JPY / gold decimal hints on the ticket.
  • Duplicate warning: same pair + entry datetime + entry price.
  • Open trades cannot affect win rate, profit factor, or equity.
  • Profit factor protected against divide-by-zero.
  • Broker PnL override always wins over the estimate — documented on the ticket.
  • Example trades are tagged EXAMPLE in notes and can be wiped without touching live data if you delete them from the log.

11. Fields that were missing from the original brief (now included)

  • Initial capital and account currency (equity cannot exist without them).
  • Commission / swap (otherwise profit factor is fiction).
  • Broker PnL override (crosses cannot be converted to USD without a second rate).
  • Open vs closed status (unrealized trades must not pollute stats).
  • Pips, planned RR vs realized RR, SL/TP distances.
  • Exit reason, plan adherence, market condition, emotion, timeframe.
  • Ticket ID, chart URL, MAE / MFE (optional, for later process work).
  • Risk % as well as risk amount.
  • Expectancy and max drawdown (desk-level, not vanity).
  • Custom setup / other pair so the system stays scalable.
  • Trader name and Trader ID (desk, ticket, exports, Excel cover).

12. Initial capital

Set it once under Settings. Every closed trade adds net PnL to a running equity series that starts at that number. Changing capital recomputes the whole curve; it does not rewrite historical trade PnL. If you deposit or withdraw, log it later as a non-trade adjustment only if you introduce one — this version treats the account as a closed trading pool plus starting cash.

How to use it day to day

  1. Settings → trader name, Trader ID, and initial capital.
  2. New trade → fill the left ticket; watch the right ticket calculate.
  3. Leave the exit blank if it is still running.
  4. When it closes, edit the same row (Trade log → pencil) and enter exit + optional broker PnL.
  5. Read the dashboard weekly; use Analytics filters to kill a pair, a weekday, or a setup that does not pay.
